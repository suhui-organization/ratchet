Privileges are compiled, not hand-written.

Ratchet reads what your agents can actually reach, compiles that into a least-privilege policy, and hands over evidence the recipient verifies on their own machine.

The recipient verifies. You don't ask them to trust you.

Every delivery carries a sha256 manifest. Hashes are recomputed on the reader's machine — in a browser, or with one standard-library script.

Open the verifier

What it does not do

  • Not a sandbox. It does not contain anything.
  • Does not block tool calls.
  • Static scan: unparsed configs are reported, never guessed.
  • Records tool names, not arguments.

Get started

Build
$ cd ratchet && make build$ make dist
Scan (reads configs, runs nothing)
$ ./bin/ratchet scan --home ~
